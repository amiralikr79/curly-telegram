```
Over the next five years, Karimi Developments aims to establish 
itself as Metro Vancouver's leading boutique infill developer—
known for design excellence, operational discipline, and the 
creation of family-scale housing in established neighborhoods.

Our pipeline strategy is built on three core principles:

**1. Geographic Focus**  
We concentrate our efforts in four strategic submarkets where zoning, 
transit access, and family demand align with our development model.

**2. Replicable Typology**  
We develop a limited set of architectural prototypes (3–6 unit 
multiplexes) that can be adapted to different sites—allowing us to 
move faster while maintaining design quality.

**3. Disciplined Growth**  
We target 3–5 projects per year, scaling gradually to preserve 
operational control and capital discipline rather than pursuing 
aggressive growth that compromises standards.

This is not a land-banking strategy. This is not speculative acquisition. 
This is a carefully sequenced pipeline of high-conviction projects that 
reflect our design philosophy and financial criteria.
```

---

**Two-Column Criteria List:**

**Left Column: Physical Attributes**

```
**Lot Characteristics:**

• Size: 7,000 – 9,000 sqft minimum  
• Dimensions: 50ft+ frontage (ideal), 120ft+ depth  
• Lane access: Preferred (simplifies parking/servicing)  
• Topography: Level or gentle slope (< 15%)  
• Trees: Mature trees acceptable (design around retention)  
• Shape: Regular geometry (avoids variance requirements)  

**Zoning & Regulatory:**

• Pre-zoned for 3–6 unit multiplex (no rezoning risk)  
• RS-3, RS-5, RT-2, or equivalent zoning  
• No heritage designation or restrictive covenants  
• Standard setback and height requirements  
• No active development permit red flags  
```

---

**Right Column: Location & Market**

```
**Location Attributes:**

• Transit: Within 800m walk of rapid transit station  
• Walkability: Walk Score 70+ (grocery, services, parks)  
• Schools: Strong elementary school catchment  
• Character: Established neighborhood (not industrial fringe)  
• Street: Quiet residential street (avoid arterials)  

**Market Fundamentals:**

• Recent comparables: $750K–$1.2M per unit (resale market)  
• Family demand: High proportion of families with children  
• Absorption: Multiple sales per quarter in submarket  
• Price trajectory: Stable to appreciating (avoid declining markets)  
• Competition: Limited new supply (low condo saturation)  
```

```
Key Risks & Mitigation Strategies
```

**Typography:**

- Margin Bottom: 48px

**Two-Column Risk Table:**

**Risk 1: Market Cycle Downturn**

**Mitigation:**
```
• Conservative underwriting (–15% pricing sensitivity)  
• Pre-sale strategies to lock in demand  
• Rental hold optionality (positive cash flow scenarios)  
• Maintain 12–18 month operating reserves  
```

---

**Risk 2: Construction Cost Escalation**

**Mitigation:**
```
• Material cost escalation buffers (7–10%)  
• Fixed-price GC contracts where possible  
• Trade relationships for priority pricing  
• Value engineering without compromising design  
```

---

**Risk 3: Permitting Delays**

**Mitigation:**
```
• Pre-zoned sites only (no rezoning risk)  
• Pre-application consultation with municipalities  
• 3–6 month timeline buffers  
• Strong consultant relationships for fast-track reviews  
```

---

**Risk 4: Operational Overextension**

**Mitigation:**
```
• Disciplined project pacing (max 5 per year)  
• Standardized processes and typologies  
• Strategic hiring as pipeline grows  
• No geographic expansion beyond four core markets  
```
