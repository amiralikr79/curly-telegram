```
Karimi Developments began with a simple belief: that small-scale 
development, executed with intention and care, could create lasting 
value—for families, for neighborhoods, and for the long-term health 
of Metro Vancouver's urban fabric.

Founded on principles borrowed from both architecture and institutional 
investing, we set out to build a different kind of development firm—one 
that operates with the precision of a capital allocator and the 
sensibility of a designer.

Our roots run deep in this region. We grew up watching Vancouver evolve 
from a collection of single-family neighborhoods into a complex, 
multi-layered city in need of thoughtful densification. We saw 
opportunities where others saw only zoning restrictions—places where 
small-scale infill could create gentle density while respecting the 
character of established communities.

Rather than chase the largest possible projects, we chose to focus on 
what we do best: boutique multiplex developments of 3–6 units, designed 
with the same level of architectural attention typically reserved for 
custom single-family homes.

We believe this scale allows us to maintain control over every detail—
from site selection and design development through permitting, 
construction, and final delivery. It allows us to move quickly, adapt 
intelligently, and build homes that reflect our values rather than 
compromise them.

This is not a portfolio assembled by committee. This is hands-on, 
operator-led development—guided by the conviction that good architecture, 
disciplined execution, and long-term thinking produce results that 
outlast market cycles.
```

Karimi Developments operates as a vertically integrated team—

site evaluation through final occupancy.

We don't believe in delegating critical thinking to consultants 
or outsourcing design decisions to the lowest bidder. Every project 
reflects our direct involvement at every stage:

**Site Acquisition:** We walk every lot personally, studying sun angles, 
tree placement, neighboring context, and access conditions before 
making an offer.

**Design Development:** We work closely with architects to iterate 
on massing, fenestration, material palettes, and interior layouts—
ensuring every decision aligns with our design standards.

**Permitting:** We navigate municipal processes with patience and 
precision, building relationships with city planners and treating 
approvals as collaborative problem-solving, not adversarial combat.

**Construction Management:** We maintain close site oversight, coordinate 
trades personally, and make real-time decisions that keep projects on 
schedule and on budget without sacrificing quality.

**Delivery:** We don't walk away at completion. We ensure every detail—
from hardware to landscaping—meets our standards before handing keys 
to new homeowners.

This is what we mean by "operator-led." We are not passive allocators 
of capital. We are builders—present, accountable, and committed to 
the outcome.

```
Metro Vancouver is at an inflection point. 

The region's housing crisis isn't solved by mega-towers alone—it requires 
a spectrum of housing types, including the often-overlooked "missing middle": 
multiplexes, townhomes, and small-scale infill that adds density without 
erasing neighborhood character.

This is where we focus our work.

We seek out underutilized lots in established neighborhoods—places where 
gentle densification can bring new families into walkable, transit-connected 
communities without displacing the qualities that made those neighborhoods 
desirable in the first place.

We believe that small-scale development, executed with care, can:

→ Provide naturally affordable homeownership options (relative to detached homes)  
→ Increase urban vitality without overwhelming infrastructure  
→ Preserve tree canopy and green space through thoughtful site planning  
→ Support local businesses by adding residents within walking distance  
→ Create architectural diversity that enriches the streetscape  

This is not about maximizing profits through brute-force density. 

It's about building the right projects in the right places—projects that 
strengthen the urban fabric rather than strain it, and that serve families 
for decades to come.

Vancouver deserves better than formulaic development. We're committed to 
delivering it—one thoughtfully designed multiplex at a time.
```
