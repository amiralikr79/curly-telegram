## Interactive Elements & Animations

### Page Load
- Hero content fades in with 40px upward drift over 800ms
- Delay stagger: Headline (0ms) → Subheadline (200ms) → Buttons (400ms)

### Scroll Animations
- Sections fade in and drift up (40px) as they enter viewport
- Trigger: When section is 20% visible
- Duration: 600ms, ease-out

### Hover States
- Buttons: Background color transition (200ms)
- Project cards: Scale 1.02x and subtle shadow increase (300ms)
- Links: Underline appears from left to right (250ms)

- Enable smooth scrolling for anchor links
