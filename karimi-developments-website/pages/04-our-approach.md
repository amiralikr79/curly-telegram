**Process Details:**

**Site Identification**

```
We focus on pre-zoned multiplex lots in established Metro Vancouver 
neighborhoods that meet our acquisition criteria:

• 7,000 – 9,000 sqft lot size  
• Existing multiplex zoning (RS-3, RS-5, or equivalent)  
• Lane access or clear frontage access  
• Transit proximity (within 800m of rapid transit)  
• Strong family demand indicators  
• Neighborhood walkability score > 70  
• Mature tree canopy preservation potential  

We walk every potential site personally—studying sun angles, neighboring 
context, topography, and access conditions before making any financial 
commitment.
```

**Typography:**

---

**Financial Underwriting**

```
Every site is modeled with conservative assumptions and stress-tested 
for downside scenarios:

**Feasibility Analysis:**  
• Land cost per buildable sqft  
• Estimated construction cost (per sqft by unit type)  
• Soft costs (design, permitting, financing, marketing)  
• Timeline assumptions (18-24 months typical)  
• Market comparables analysis  
• Absorption rate modeling  

**Risk Assessment:**  
• Construction cost escalation sensitivity (±10%)  
• Market pricing downside scenarios (–15% / –25%)  
• Permitting delay contingencies (3-6 month buffer)  
• Interest rate sensitivity analysis  
• Exit strategy flexibility (hold vs. sell scenarios)  

We use a target IRR of 18-22% on equity, with minimum 1.4x equity 
multiple. Projects that don't meet our return threshold—regardless of 
how compelling the site—don't move forward.
```

**Typography:** (Same as above)

---

**Due Diligence**

```
Once a site meets our acquisition criteria, we conduct comprehensive 
due diligence:

• Title search and legal review  
• Geotechnical assessment (soil reports, bearing capacity)  
• Environmental Phase I assessment  
• Survey and boundary confirmation  
• Utility capacity verification  
• Municipal zoning verification  
• Existing structure condition report (if applicable)  
• Arborist report for protected trees  
• Neighborhood consultation planning  

We don't close until every material risk has been identified and 
mitigation strategies are in place.
```

---

**Process Details:**

**Design Development**

```
We work with a curated network of Vancouver-based architects who 
share our commitment to design excellence and constructability.

**Conceptual Design Phase:**  
• Site analysis and solar studies  
• Massing exploration (multiple options)  
• Zoning envelope optimization  
• Unit mix and program development  
• Material palette selection  
• Preliminary cost estimating  

**Schematic Design Phase:**  
• Refined floor plans and elevations  
• Natural light modeling (daylight factor analysis)  
• Material specifications and detailing  
• Landscape integration strategy  
• Privacy and acoustic planning  
• Preliminary structural coordination  

**Design Development Phase:**  
• Construction documentation coordination  
• Mechanical, electrical, plumbing integration  
• Building envelope performance modeling  
• Accessibility compliance review  
• Energy code compliance (Step Code targets)  
• Final cost estimation and value engineering  

We iterate until the design meets three criteria: architectural 
intent, budget constraints, and municipal approval probability.
```

---

**Municipal Permitting Strategy**

```
We approach permitting as a collaborative process, not an adversarial one.

**Pre-Application Consultation:**  
• Meet with city planning staff before formal submission  
• Address potential concerns proactively  
• Align design with municipal design guidelines  
• Build relationships with assigned planners  

**Development Permit Application:**  
• Complete architectural drawings package  
• Landscape and arborist reports  
• Parking and transportation studies  
• Shadow impact analysis  
• Neighborhood context elevations  
• Public consultation materials (if required)  

**Building Permit Coordination:**  
• Structural engineering coordination  
• Building code compliance review  
• Energy modeling and compliance reports  
• Third-party review responses  
• Trade permit coordination  

**Timeline Management:**  
We budget 6-9 months for full permit approval but maintain flexibility 
for revision requests. Our goal is approval on first submission—which 
requires thorough preparation and anticipation of planner concerns.
```

**Process Details:**

**Pre-Construction Planning**

```
Before breaking ground, we establish the framework for successful execution:

• General contractor procurement (competitive bid or negotiated)  
• Trade subcontractor pre-qualification  
• Material procurement and lead time management  
• Construction schedule development (detailed Gantt chart)  
• Site logistics planning (staging, access, waste management)  
• Quality control protocols and inspection schedule  
• Safety compliance and WorkSafeBC coordination  
• Neighbor communication plan  
```

---

**Construction Management**

```
We maintain active involvement throughout construction:

**Weekly Site Walks:**  
• Visual progress verification  
• Quality inspection (framing, envelope, finishes)  
• Trade coordination review  
• Safety compliance checks  

**Cost Control:**  
• Monthly draw review and budget tracking  
• Change order evaluation and approval  
• Contingency management (5-7% typical)  
• Value engineering opportunities  

**Schedule Management:**  
• Critical path monitoring  
• Trade sequencing coordination  
• Weather delay mitigation  
• Permit inspection coordination  

**Quality Assurance:**  
• Material verification (confirm spec compliance)  
• Building envelope testing (blower door, thermal imaging)  
• Millwork and finish inspection  
• Deficiency tracking and resolution  
```

---

**Construction Timeline & Milestones**

```
Typical 12-14 Month Construction Schedule:

**Months 1-2:** Site prep, demo (if applicable), excavation, foundations  
**Months 3-5:** Framing, roof, rough-ins (mechanical/electrical/plumbing)  
**Months 6-8:** Building envelope, windows, exterior cladding  
**Months 9-11:** Insulation, drywall, interior finishes, cabinetry  
**Months 12-14:** Final fixtures, landscaping, final inspections, occupancy  

We buffer timelines conservatively and communicate delays transparently.
```
