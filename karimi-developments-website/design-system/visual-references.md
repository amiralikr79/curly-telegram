# Visual References & Image Specifications

---

## Photography Style Guide

### Architectural Photography Requirements

**Technical Specifications:**
- **Resolution:** Minimum 2400px on longest edge
- **Format:** JPEG (web), TIFF (source files)
- **Color Space:** sRGB for web, Adobe RGB for print
- **Compression:** 85% quality for web optimization
- **Aspect Ratios:** 
  - Hero Images: 16:9 or 21:9 (cinematic)
  - Portfolio: 4:3 or 3:2
  - Detail Shots: 1:1 (square) or 4:5 (portrait)

**Lighting Conditions:**
- Golden hour (1 hour after sunrise, 1 hour before sunset)
- Overcast days for even, soft light
- Blue hour for twilight ambiance (use sparingly)
- Avoid harsh midday sun

**Color Grading:**
- Slight warm cast (+5-10K color temperature)
- Reduced saturation (-10 to -15%)
- Lifted shadows (+10 to +20)
- Matte finish (reduce contrast curve)
- Subtle film grain (2-5% intensity)

## Image Categories & Usage

### 1. Hero Images (Homepage, About, Philosophy)

**Purpose:** Create immediate emotional connection and set brand tone

**Subjects:**
- Wide-angle architectural exteriors
- Large interior spaces with abundant natural light
- Material detail close-ups at human scale
- West Coast landscape context (forests, mountains)

**Composition:**
- Generous negative space (sky, walls, floors)
- Leading lines toward vanishing points
- Asymmetric balance
- Horizon line at 1/3 or 2/3 height

**Example Descriptions:**

1. **"Architectural Serenity"**
   - Subject: Modern wood-clad residence, partial view, surrounded by evergreens
   - Time: Late afternoon, warm directional light
   - Mood: Calm, grounded, quietly confident
   - Composition: Structure occupies right 40% of frame, forest left 60%

2. **"Material Intersection"**
   - Subject: Close-up of wood siding meeting concrete foundation
   - Focus: Texture, shadow play, craftsmanship
   - Mood: Tactile, honest, refined
   - Composition: Diagonal division, shallow depth of field

3. **"Light Volume"**
   - Subject: Minimalist interior, empty room with large windows
   - Light: Dramatic side light creating geometric shadows
   - Mood: Contemplative, spacious, human-scaled
   - Composition: Centered window, symmetrical balance

---

### 2. Portfolio Project Renderings

**Purpose:** Showcase design vision through conceptual visualizations

**Rendering Style:**
- Photorealistic but not hyper-polished
- Natural lighting simulation (no artificial studio lights)
- Environmental context included (site, vegetation, sky)
- Slightly soft focus acceptable (mimics photography)
- People optional (if included: faceless, motion-blurred silhouettes)

**Required Views per Project:**

**Exterior Views:**
1. Street-level perspective (human eye height, 5'6")
2. Aerial oblique (45° angle, showing roof and context)
3. Detail shot (material junction, window detail, entry sequence)

**Interior Views:**
1. Living space with natural light
2. Kitchen or dining area
3. Stairway or circulation space (if applicable)

**Material Accuracy:**
- Wood: Show grain direction, natural variation
- Concrete: Subtle form texture, realistic tone variation
- Glass: Proper reflections and refraction
- Metal: Matte or brushed finish (no chrome)

**Atmospheric Elements:**
- Soft clouds or clear sky (no dramatic sunsets)
- Trees with realistic foliage density
- Ground plane with subtle landscape treatment
- Depth of field: f/8 to f/11 equivalent

---

### 3. Detail & Texture Photography

**Purpose:** Highlight material quality and craftsmanship

**Subjects:**
- Wood grain close-ups (cedar, douglas fir, hemlock)
- Concrete surface texture
- Window frame details
- Hardware and fixtures
- Stairway geometry
- Shadow patterns on walls

**Composition:**
- Macro or close-up focal length
- Shallow depth of field (f/2.8 to f/5.6)
- Fill frame with subject
- Abstract enough to be interpretive

**Color Palette:**
- Warm wood tones: honey, caramel, amber
- Cool concrete tones: dove gray, slate, bone
- Metal accents: blackened steel, brushed bronze
- Glass: Clear with subtle blue-green tint

---

### 4. Lifestyle Context (Use Sparingly)

**Purpose:** Suggest inhabitability without being prescriptive

**Approach:**
- Implied presence (coffee cup on counter, book on chair)
- Never show faces directly
- Motion blur if people are included
- Always secondary to architecture

**Acceptable Lifestyle Elements:**
- Reading nook with daylight
- Dining table set for meal
- Outdoor terrace with simple furniture
- Workspace with natural light

**Avoid:**
- Staged family photos
- Obviously styled interiors
- Trendy decor that will date
- People looking at camera

## Image Treatment & Post-Processing

### Standard Adjustments (Apply to All Images):

**Color:**
- Temperature: +5 to +10 (warm)
- Tint: Neutral to +5 (slightly magenta)
- Saturation: -10 to -15 (muted)
- Vibrance: +5 to +10 (subtle lift)

**Tone:**
- Exposure: Adjust for consistent brightness across site
- Highlights: -10 to -20 (protect blown areas)
- Shadows: +10 to +20 (lift darkness)
- Whites: +5 (subtle lift)
- Blacks: -5 to -10 (slight crush)

**Detail:**
- Clarity: +5 to +10 (subtle micro-contrast)
- Texture: +5 (gentle enhancement)
- Sharpening: 40-60 (standard)
- Noise Reduction: As needed per ISO

**Effects:**
- Vignette: -5 to -10 (subtle darkening)
- Grain: +2 to +5 (film-like texture)
- Dehaze: 0 to +5 (if needed)

### Format & Optimization:

**For Web Use:**
- Export as JPEG, 85% quality
- Resize to maximum 2400px width
- Apply slight sharpening after resize
- Use CSS lazy loading
- Serve WebP format with JPEG fallback

**File Naming Convention:**
```
karimi-[category]-[description]-[number].jpg

Examples:
karimi-hero-architecture-01.jpg
karimi-portfolio-willow-exterior-01.jpg
karimi-detail-wood-concrete-01.jpg
karimi-about-team-01.jpg
```

## Image Placement Strategy by Page

### Homepage:
- **Hero:** 1 full-width image (architectural exterior or dramatic interior)
- **Philosophy Preview:** 3 small square images (material details)
- **Portfolio Teaser:** 2-3 project images (1200x900px each)
- **Total:** 5-7 images

### About:
- **Hero:** 1 medium-width image (team context or office detail)
- **Story Section:** 2-3 supporting images (office, work process, site visit)
- **Total:** 3-4 images

### Philosophy:
- **Hero:** 1 full-width poetic image (light and shadow)
- **Section Breaks:** 3-4 atmospheric images between manifesto sections
- **Total:** 4-5 images

### Our Approach:
- **Header:** 1 image (process-oriented: plans, site, materials)
- **Process Sections:** 4 small images (one per phase)
- **Total:** 5 images

### Portfolio (Per Project):
- **Hero:** 1 large exterior rendering
- **Gallery:** 4-6 additional views (mix of exterior/interior)
- **Total per project:** 5-7 images
- **Total portfolio page:** 15-21 images

### Pipeline Strategy:
- **Header:** 1 map-style graphic or aerial site image
- **Supporting:** 2-3 location context images
- **Total:** 3-4 images

### Investor Relations:
- **Header:** 1 professional image (office, documentation, or abstract)
- **Supporting:** Minimal (1-2 charts or diagrams)
- **Total:** 2-3 images

### Contact:
- **Background:** 1 subtle texture or abstract image (optional)
- **Total:** 0-1 images
