# Karimi Developments — Brand Guidelines

---

## Brand Identity

**Company Name:** Karimi Developments  
**Tagline:** Design. Discipline. Legacy.  
**Brand Essence:** Calm. Minimal. Architectural. Disciplined.

**Positioning Statement:**  
Karimi Developments is a next-generation boutique development firm building legacy-scale homes through intention, precision, and architectural stewardship in Metro Vancouver's evolving infill landscape.

## Visual Identity

### Logo Specifications

**Primary Wordmark:**
- Typeface: Custom geometric sans (recommend: Neue Haas Grotesk, Suisse Int'l, or GT America)
- Weight: Light or Regular
- Letter spacing: +50 tracking (generous breathing room)
- Case: All caps or Title Case
- Color: Charcoal (#2B2B2B) on light backgrounds, Cream (#F4F1EA) on dark

**Secondary Lockup:**
- Stacked version with tagline below
- Horizontal divider line (1px, 40% opacity)

**Minimum Clear Space:** Equal to height of capital "K"

## Typography

### Primary Typeface (Headings)
**Serif:** 
- Recommended: Tiempos Text, Freight Display, or Cormorant Garamond
- Use for: Hero headlines, section titles, featured quotes
- Weights: Light (300), Regular (400)
- Style: Elegant, editorial, refined

**Weights & Usage:**
- Light (300): Large display headlines (Hero sections)
- Regular (400): H1, H2, featured statements

### Secondary Typeface (Body + UI)
**Geometric Sans:**
- Recommended: Inter, Neue Haas Grotesk, Suisse Int'l
- Use for: Body copy, navigation, buttons, captions, microcopy
- Weights: Regular (400), Medium (500)
- Style: Clean, modern, highly readable

**Weights & Usage:**
- Regular (400): Body text, paragraph copy
- Medium (500): Buttons, navigation, labels

### Type Scale

```
Display (Hero):     48-72px / Line height 1.1 / Serif Light
H1:                 36-48px / Line height 1.2 / Serif Regular
H2:                 28-36px / Line height 1.3 / Serif Regular
H3:                 20-24px / Line height 1.4 / Sans Medium
Body Large:         18-20px / Line height 1.6 / Sans Regular
Body Regular:       16-18px / Line height 1.7 / Sans Regular
Caption/Microcopy:  12-14px / Line height 1.5 / Sans Regular
```

**Muted Gold**  
- Hex: `#C9A961`
- RGB: 201, 169, 97
- Usage: Hover states, subtle highlights, luxury accents (use sparingly)

**Soft Black**  
- Hex: `#1A1A1A`
- RGB: 26, 26, 26

### Usage Guidelines

- **Backgrounds:** Off-White or Cream as primary, Charcoal for hero sections
- **Text:** Charcoal on light backgrounds, Cream on dark backgrounds
- **Dividers:** Stone Gray at 20-30% opacity
- **Accents:** Muted Gold for CTAs or hover states only
- **Never use:** Pure black (#000000) or pure white (#FFFFFF)

## Photography & Imagery

### Style Direction

**Aesthetic:**
- Minimalist architectural photography
- Natural light emphasis
- Clean, uncluttered compositions
- Warm, muted tones
- Human scale but often people-free
- West Coast sensibility (forest, mountain, ocean context)

**Color Treatment:**
- Slightly desaturated
- Warm highlights, cool shadows
- Matte finish (avoid high gloss)
- Film-like grain acceptable

**Subject Matter:**
- Modern West Coast architecture
- Natural wood + concrete details
- Interior spaces with abundant light
- Material close-ups (wood grain, concrete texture)
- Site context (trees, sky, distant mountains)
- Empty rooms that feel inhabited
- Proportional geometry and shadow play

### Rendering Style (for Portfolio Projects)

**For Conceptual Projects:**
- High-quality architectural visualization
- Soft, natural daylight (golden hour preferred)
- Realistic materiality
- Subtle atmospheric depth
- Not hyper-polished — should feel grounded and real
- Include context (trees, landscape, sky)

**Rendering Software Recommendations:**
- V-Ray, Corona, Lumion, Enscape, or Twinmotion
- Avoid overly glossy or futuristic aesthetics

### Image Composition Rules

1. **Horizon Lines:** Keep level and thoughtfully placed
2. **Symmetry:** Embrace when architectural, break when human
3. **Negative Space:** Generous — let images breathe
4. **Contrast:** Subtle, not extreme
5. **Focus:** Soft depth of field acceptable, but maintain clarity

## Design Principles

### 1. Restraint Over Excess
Less is always more. Remove before adding.

### 2. Hierarchy Through Space
Use whitespace, not decoration, to create visual hierarchy.

### 3. Material Honesty
Show real materials. Avoid artificial gloss or synthetic textures.

### 4. Calm Over Flash
Movement should be subtle. Avoid aggressive animations or transitions.

### 5. Timelessness Over Trend
Design choices should age gracefully over 5-10 years.

## Grid & Layout

### Grid System
- **Columns:** 12-column grid
- **Gutter:** 32px desktop, 24px tablet, 16px mobile
- **Margins:** 80px desktop, 40px tablet, 24px mobile
- **Max Width:** 1440px for content container

### Section Spacing
- **Between Sections:** 120px desktop, 80px tablet, 60px mobile
- **Within Sections:** 60px desktop, 40px tablet, 32px mobile
- **Element Spacing:** 24px standard, 16px tight

### Layout Patterns
- **Asymmetric Balance:** Preferred over perfect symmetry
- **Full-Bleed Images:** Use strategically for impact
- **Cards:** Subtle elevation (2-4px shadow), rounded corners (8px)
- **Dividers:** Minimal use, 1px Stone Gray at 20% opacity

## Animation & Interaction

### Principles
- **Subtle:** All animations should feel barely noticeable
- **Purposeful:** Animate to guide attention, not decorate
- **Speed:** 200-400ms transitions
- **Easing:** Ease-out for entrances, ease-in for exits

### Recommended Animations
- **Page Load:** Fade in with subtle upward drift (40px, 600ms)
- **Hover:** Scale 1.02x or slight opacity shift (200ms)
- **Scroll:** Parallax at 0.3x speed (use sparingly)
- **Transitions:** Crossfade between pages (400ms)

### What to Avoid
- ❌ Aggressive parallax
- ❌ Spinning or rotating elements
- ❌ Bounce effects
- ❌ Auto-playing video with sound
- ❌ Excessive cursor effects

## Voice & Tone

### Vocabulary Guidelines

**Use These Words:**
- Intentional, disciplined, considered, thoughtful
- Legacy, stewardship, enduring, timeless
- Craft, precision, care, rigor
- Human-scale, proportional, grounded
- Calm, quiet, restrained

**Avoid These Words:**
- Luxury, premium, exclusive (too sales-y)
- Disruptive, innovative, revolutionary (too tech-bro)
- Iconic, stunning, breathtaking (too hyperbolic)
- Investment opportunity (too transactional)

### Sentence Structure
- **Length:** Vary between short and medium. Avoid run-ons.
- **Rhythm:** Create breathing room between ideas.
- **Style:** Declarative statements. Minimal questions.
- **Punctuation:** Em dashes and semicolons acceptable; ellipses rarely.

### Example Copy (Correct Tone)

> "We build for the long game. Each project begins with questions of light, proportion, and human scale — the elements that outlast trends and ground architecture in place. Our work is quiet by design. Intentional by discipline. Enduring by choice."

## Do's and Don'ts

### ✓ Do
- Embrace negative space
- Use high-quality imagery sparingly
- Let typography create hierarchy
- Maintain visual calm
- Show restraint in color usage
- Design for longevity

### ✗ Don't
- Clutter layouts with excessive elements
- Use trendy design patterns that will date quickly
- Over-animate or create visual noise
- Use aggressive CTAs or sales language
- Compromise on image quality
- Rush the design process
