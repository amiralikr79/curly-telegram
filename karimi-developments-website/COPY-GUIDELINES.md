## Brand Voice

### Core Attributes

**Karimi Developments speaks with a voice that is:**

1. **Confident, not arrogant**
   - We state our expertise clearly without boasting
   - We demonstrate competence through specificity, not superlatives

2. **Minimal, not sparse**
   - We use fewer words but make each one count
   - We embrace white space in design and pauses in rhythm

3. **Philosophical, not pretentious**
   - We articulate principles without being preachy
   - We ground ideas in tangible examples

4. **Technical, not jargon-heavy**
   - We explain our process with precision
   - We avoid insider terminology that alienates readers

5. **Warm, not sentimental**
   - We acknowledge human needs without emotional manipulation
   - We focus on function and beauty, not nostalgia

## Tone Variations by Context

### Website Pages

**Homepage, About, Portfolio:**
- Tone: Welcoming, confident, design-forward
- Sentence Length: Mix of short and medium (10-20 words average)
- Paragraphs: 3-5 sentences maximum
- Purpose: Build connection, establish credibility

**Philosophy:**
- Tone: Contemplative, poetic, authoritative
- Sentence Length: Varied (5-25 words, deliberate rhythm)
- Paragraphs: 4-7 sentences acceptable for flow
- Purpose: Articulate principles, inspire alignment

**Our Approach, Pipeline Strategy:**
- Tone: Professional, process-oriented, precise
- Sentence Length: Medium (12-18 words)
- Paragraphs: 3-4 sentences, organized with subheadings
- Purpose: Demonstrate competence, build confidence

**Investor Relations:**
- Tone: Institutional, transparent, data-informed
- Sentence Length: Medium-long (15-22 words)
- Paragraphs: 4-6 sentences, dense with information
- Purpose: Communicate returns, risk management, governance

**Contact:**
- Tone: Accessible, helpful, professional
- Sentence Length: Short-medium (8-15 words)
- Paragraphs: 2-3 sentences
- Purpose: Facilitate communication, set expectations

### Avoid These Words & Phrases

**Overused Real Estate Jargon:**
- ❌ "World-class" (too vague, overused)
- ❌ "Best-in-class" (too sales-y)
- ❌ "Cutting-edge" (too tech-bro)
- ❌ "Iconic" (too hyperbolic)
- ❌ "Stunning" (lazy adjective)

**Luxury/Premium Language:**
- ❌ "Luxury" (loaded term, avoid unless context-specific)
- ❌ "Exclusive" (elitist connotation)
- ❌ "High-end" (use "high-quality" instead)
- ❌ "Prestigious" (too status-focused)

**Speculative/Aggressive Language:**
- ❌ "Ground-floor opportunity" (MLM vibes)
- ❌ "Don't miss out" (creates false urgency)
- ❌ "Limited time" (unless genuinely true)
- ❌ "Once-in-a-lifetime" (hyperbolic)

**Vague Modifiers:**
- ❌ "Very" (use stronger base word)
- ❌ "Really" (filler word)
- ❌ "Quite" (weakens statement)
- ❌ "Pretty" (informal, vague)

## Sentence Structure

### Guidelines

**Vary Sentence Length:**
- Short sentences create impact. They command attention.
- Medium sentences provide explanation and connect ideas smoothly across clauses.
- Longer sentences can build complexity and rhythm when used strategically, allowing for nuance and the layering of concepts that might otherwise feel disjointed if broken apart.

**Average:** Mix all three types in each paragraph for natural rhythm.

**Avoid:**
- Run-on sentences (30+ words without clear breaks)
- Too many short sentences in a row (feels choppy)
- Starting every sentence the same way

---

### Sentence Opening Variety

**Don't Always Start with Subject:**

❌ Weak (repetitive):
```
We design homes for families.
We focus on natural light.
We select durable materials.
```

✓ Better (varied):
```
We design homes for families. Natural light shapes every decision. 
Materials are chosen for durability, not fashion.
```

**Use Occasional Fragments for Emphasis:**
```
This is not speculation. This is stewardship.
```

**Strategic Use of Lists:**
- Use bullets for clarity and scanability
- Keep parallel structure (all bullets start same way)
- Limit to 5-7 items maximum

---

## Paragraph Structure

### Guidelines

**Opening Sentences:**
- State the paragraph's main idea clearly
- Use active voice when possible
- Make the first sentence compelling (earn the read)

**Body Sentences:**
- Support the opening with evidence, examples, or explanation
- Use transitions to connect ideas ("However," "Because," "This means")
- Vary sentence structure within paragraph

**Closing Sentences:**
- Conclude the idea or bridge to next paragraph
- Create rhythm and forward momentum
- Avoid repetitive closers ("This is why...", "That's how...")

**Paragraph Length:**
- Website: 3-5 sentences maximum (scannable)
- Philosophy/Manifesto: 4-7 sentences acceptable (rhythmic reading)
- Investor Relations: 4-6 sentences (information-dense)

## Grammar & Style Rules

### Punctuation

**Em Dashes (—):**
- Use for emphasis or aside thoughts
- Don't overuse (max 2-3 per section)
- Example: "This is not about trends—it's about timelessness."

**Semicolons (;):**
- Use to connect related independent clauses
- Acceptable in formal/institutional content
- Example: "We design with intention; we execute with discipline."

**Colons (:):**
- Use to introduce lists or explanations
- Example: "We focus on three principles: design, discipline, legacy."

**Ellipses (...):**
- Avoid in formal content (use em dashes instead)
- Acceptable only in conversational microcopy

**Oxford Comma:**
- Always use (clarity over style)
- Example: "wood, concrete, and glass" ✓

---

### Numbers & Statistics

**Spell Out or Use Numerals:**
- One through nine: spell out ("three units")
- 10 and above: use numerals ("15 years")
- Exception: Technical specs always use numerals ("7,500 sqft")

**Ranges:**
- Use en dash (–) for ranges: "3–6 units" or "18–22% IRR"
- Include spaces around en dash in text, no spaces in technical specs

**Percentages:**
- Always use numeral + % symbol: "22% IRR" (not "twenty-two percent")

**Measurements:**
- Use abbreviations: sqft, psf, ft, m
- Include comma for thousands: "7,500 sqft" not "7500 sqft"

**Currency:**
- Use $ symbol: "$1.8M" or "$35M–$50M"
- Round to nearest significant figure (avoid "$1,847,293.12" in prose)

## Capitalization

### Title Case vs. Sentence Case

**Title Case (For Headlines):**
- Capitalize: All major words (nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs)
- Lowercase: Articles (a, an, the), conjunctions (and, but, or), prepositions under 5 letters (in, on, at, to)
- Example: "Building the Next Generation of Metro Vancouver Housing"

**Sentence Case (For Subheadings, Body):**
- Capitalize: Only first word and proper nouns
- Example: "We design homes for three generations"

**All Caps (Use Sparingly):**
- Navigation labels: Optional (looks clean)
- Section labels: Optional for overlines ("OUR PHILOSOPHY")
- Never: Full paragraphs or headlines (hard to read)

---

### Brand Terms

**Always Capitalize:**
- Karimi Developments (full company name)
- Metro Vancouver (proper place name)
- West Coast Modern (architectural style, when used as proper noun)

**Lowercase (Common Terms):**
- boutique development
- multiplex, infill, terrace
- courtyard, split-level
- capital stack, equity, returns

## Calls to Action (CTAs)

### Button Copy Guidelines

**Keep Short:** 1-4 words ideal

**Use Action Verbs:**
- ✓ "Explore Our Work"
- ✓ "Get in Touch"
- ✓ "View Portfolio"
- ✓ "Request Information"

**Avoid Generic:**
- ❌ "Click Here"
- ❌ "Learn More" (unless paired with context)
- ❌ "Submit" (use "Send Message" instead)

**Be Specific:**
- ✓ "Request Investor Information Pack" (better than "Learn More")
- ✓ "See How We Work" (better than "Our Approach")

---

### Link Text Guidelines

**Use Descriptive Anchor Text:**

❌ Weak:
```
For more information about our philosophy, click here.
```

✓ Better:
```
Read our full design philosophy.
```

**Make Links Scannable:**
- Use arrow (→) to indicate forward movement
- Example: "View Our Projects →"
- Or: "Investor Information →"

---

## Microcopy

### Form Labels

**Be Clear and Specific:**
- ✓ "Full Name" (not "Name")
- ✓ "Email Address" (not "Email")
- ✓ "I'm reaching out as a..." (not "Type")

**Use Helpful Placeholders:**
- "your.email@example.com"
- "Brief subject line (optional)"
- "Tell us about your inquiry..."

**Error Messages:**
- Be polite and helpful
- ✓ "Please enter a valid email address"
- ❌ "Error: Invalid input"

---

### Image Captions

**Use Sparingly:**
- Only when image needs context
- Keep to 1 sentence or phrase
- Italic, smaller font, Stone Gray color
- Example: "Light as material. Shadow as structure."

**Don't Caption:**
- Obvious images (no need to say "Modern kitchen" under kitchen photo)
- Decorative or abstract images

## SEO Copy Integration

### Natural Keyword Usage

**Target Keywords (Examples):**
- Metro Vancouver development
- Boutique infill developer
- Multiplex housing
- West Coast modern architecture
- Real estate development Vancouver

**Integration Guidelines:**
- Use naturally in headers and first paragraphs
- Don't force awkward phrasing for SEO
- Prioritize readability over keyword density
- Use variations and synonyms

**Avoid Keyword Stuffing:**

❌ Bad:
```
Karimi Developments is a Metro Vancouver development firm specializing 
in Metro Vancouver multiplex development. Our Metro Vancouver infill 
projects are the best Metro Vancouver housing developments.
```

✓ Good:
```
Karimi Developments is a boutique development firm focused on multiplex 
infill projects across Metro Vancouver. We create family-scale homes 
through architectural intention and capital discipline.
```

## Voice Checklist

When reviewing copy, ask:

**Is it confident?**
- Does it state expertise clearly without hedging or boasting?

**Is it minimal?**
- Can any words be removed without losing meaning?

**Is it specific?**
- Does it use concrete details instead of vague generalities?

**Is it warm?**
- Does it acknowledge human needs without being sentimental?

**Is it clear?**
- Would a non-expert understand it easily?

If yes to all five: it's on-brand. ✓
